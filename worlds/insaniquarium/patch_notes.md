### New YAML Options

- **Death Link** (`death_link`) - Toggle Death Link integration.
- **Death Link Fish Threshold** (`death_link_amnesty`) - Configures how many fish must die before sending a Death Link.
- **Death Link Receive Effect** (`death_link_effect`) - Configures the penalty when receiving a Death Link (kill all fish, activate trap, or random).
